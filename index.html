Research interests

Symplectic geometry, contact geometry, Floer theory, and Legendrian contact homology.

My work concerns Legendrian submanifolds and the invariants that distinguish them. Legendrian contact homology is built by counting holomorphic curves, and it carries a great deal of information about a Legendrian that is invisible to classical invariants. I am interested both in the algebraic structure of these invariants and in what they say about the geometry and topology of Legendrian knots and their front projections.
